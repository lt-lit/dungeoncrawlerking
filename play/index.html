The Gods

Earthquakes stir the arena when the game goes stale: walls crack, crates shatter, pieces scoot, squares collapse. Temperament and intensity are dialled in the debug panel (enable the overlay below); custom knob values live here and apply live mid-duel.

Replay log

Every duel records its moves, the board after every ply, the enemy's evaluations, the gods' rolls, probes and rejected draws, every undo (with the board right before it) and your flags (⚑ in the top bar). Export shares the file on a phone and downloads it on a desktop; the last three duels are kept on this device and can be exported from the stage picker.
